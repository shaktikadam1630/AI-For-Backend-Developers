## Introduction

Every backend developer eventually hits the same moment: a ticket comes in that says "add an AI chatbot" or "let users search by meaning, not keywords" — and suddenly you're expected to know things nobody taught you in school or on the job.

This isn't a data science course. You're not training models or doing research. You're doing what you already do — building software — except now one of the services you call happens to generate text instead of returning fixed rows from a database.

These notes walk through exactly what that requires: a diagram to see each idea, a plain explanation to understand it, and real code to prove it works. No Python, no math background, no shortcuts skipped.

Start here: **The AI Hierarchy** — what AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and LLMs each mean, and how they fit inside one another.
